What We Offer

SERVICES WE OFFER

Water Treatment Plant (WTP)

Removing impurities from raw water using advanced purification technologies including Reverse Osmosis (RO), Demineralization, Ultra Filtration (UF), UV Systems, Industrial Water Softeners, Iron Removal Filters, Fabricated Pressure Vessels, and Water Clarifiers.

Effluent Treatment Plant (ETP)

Specialized industrial wastewater treatment systems designed for pollution control compliance, featuring Moving Bed Bio Reactor (MBBR), Membrane Bio Reactor (MBR), Sequence Batch Reactor (SBR), UASB, Bio-towers, SAFFR, and Activated Sludge Process.

Sewage Treatment Plant (STP)

Efficient domestic and residential wastewater recycling systems utilizing Moving Bed Bio Reactor (MBBR), Membrane Bio Reactor (MBR), Sequence Batch Reactor (SBR), Activated Sludge Process, and Skid Mounted STP units.

Operation & Maintenance (O&M)

Complete lifecycle management through consultation, operation, and maintenance services in the treatment of drinking and industrial water, municipal sewage plants, and industrial wastewater treatment plants.

Environmental Consultancy

Preparation and submission of environment audit reports and hazardous waste annual reports, along with environmental monitoring services including ambient air monitoring, stack monitoring, noise monitoring, DG set insertion loss, illumination survey, work zone air monitoring, and effluent/sewage water analysis.
