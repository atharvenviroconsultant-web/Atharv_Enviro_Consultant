Who We Are

About Atharv Enviro Consultant

Atharv Enviro Consultant is a trusted name amongst environmental solution provider companies in Pune (Maharashtra), working in manufacturing, supplying, and exporting water & wastewater treatment plants and equipment across India as well as foreign countries.

We offer effective one-stop water treatment solutions for commercial, industrial, municipal, and residential wastewater. With state-of-the-art infrastructure, we manufacture Reverse Osmosis (RO) Plants, Demineralization Plants, Ultra Filtration Plants, UV Systems, Industrial Water Softeners, Pressure Sand Filters, Activated Carbon Filters, MBBR/SBR/MBR Plants, Oil Skimmers, DAF Systems, Air Diffusers, Tube Settlers, and Skid-mounted ETP/STP units.

On-Site Assistance: We provide comprehensive and experienced on-site assistance custom-tailored to meet your requirements and reduce operational costs.

Our Mission

Atharv Enviro Consultant is dedicated to bringing the most valuable solutions and providing the best customer experience in meeting water and environmental needs through innovative technology solutions, products, and services by making deep positive contributions to industry, society, and the environment.

Our Vision

Environment preservation and conservation is the need of the hour. We believe it is our moral responsibility to return a better planet to our future generations and endeavor to deliver technologies, products, and services that achieve this vision.
